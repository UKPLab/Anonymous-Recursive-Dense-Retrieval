Combinations

One encoder, every search, every readout

Dense retrieval, pseudo-relevance feedback, refinement chains, beam retrieval and document-as-query expansion differ in two choices: which states the encoder sees (the search) and how their scores become one ranking (the readout). In rdr both are arguments of one call, so each prior family is one combination, and RDR is the learned tree read out globally. Pick a combination to see the call and what the paper measured for it.

Figure 2 of the paper. Click a family to load it into the configurator below. (a) Pseudo-relevance feedback encodes observations jointly into one query vector. (b) As in DC-PRF, a refinement chain reads out its last state, interpolated with the root. (c) Beam retrievers return realized paths, each document scored by its own chain. (d) Promoting retrieved documents to queries fuses parallel lists by rank. (e) RDR scores every retained candidate with all learned states. Change over the root query in multi-hop Recall@5 and single-hop nDCG@10; (c) is BeamDR on a 5-wide beam.
