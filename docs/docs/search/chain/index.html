Search strategies

Chain search

One state per level: the root selects its best document, the child observes it and selects the next, and so on. ChainSearch(depth=H) is TreeSearch(H, branching=1). It is the refinement chain of DC-PRF. Read out globally instead of by its last state, it is the most efficient instantiation of RDR.

A chain of four states: every level encodes one state and selects one document.

The readout decides

The same four chain states give very different rankings depending on the readout (Table 30, Recall@5). Reading only the last state loses the root's evidence. Interpolating it with the root, as DC-PRF does, recovers part of it. Reading every state globally keeps all of it:

Four chained encodings reach 80.72 Recall@5 on MuSiQue at 0.15 s per question (Table 18). On single-hop suites the chain with the global readout reaches 57.95 mean nDCG@10, against 54.31 for its terminal state and 57.57 for DC-PRF's interpolation (Tables 3b, 24, 27).

Branching adds significant Recall@5 over the aggregated chain on BrowseComp+ (+2.14) and FRAMES (+3.32), but not on MuSiQue or FanOutQA (Appendix H). Readouts for chains are on Realized paths and Interpolation & Assembly.

classrdr.ChainSearch(depth=4, dedup='level')

A single chain of H states: TreeSearch(H, branching=1).

Parameters

  • depthintdefault 4

    H, encoded states of the chain including the root (paper: 4).

Advanced parameters (1)
  • dedupstrdefault 'level'

    Selection rule, as in TreeSearch; with one state per level "level" and "sequential" coincide (the chain never revisits a selected document).
