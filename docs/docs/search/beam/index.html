Search strategies

Beam search

The root selects W documents. At every level each surviving path proposes its W best candidates that were not selected before. Proposals of the same document keep the path with the highest product of raw cosines, and the W best paths by that product survive. This is the search of MDR and BeamDR, run with RDR's encoder.

The global readout stays the default: a beam is just another way to form states. The outputs that beam retrievers return, complete chains ordered by their own score, are realized readouts. You request them explicitly (Realized paths).

rdr.BeamSearch

"beam"guide[source]
classrdr.BeamSearch(width=5, depth=4, proposals=None)

Beam search over observation paths, as in MDR / BeamDR (Appendix H.1).

The root selects W candidates. At every level each path proposes its W best candidates not selected before; proposals of one document keep the path with the highest product of raw cosines, and the W best paths survive. Every proposed document counts as selected. A width-W beam has at most 1 + (H-1) W states (W = 13 matches the 40-state ceiling of RDR(4, 3)).

The default readout stays GlobalReadout; the beam retrievers' own outputs are ProbabilityChain (BeamDR) and SequenceSum (MDR).

Parameters

  • widthintdefault 5

    W, surviving paths per level; also the root's selections.

  • depthintdefault 4

    H, encoded levels including the root.

Advanced parameters (1)
  • proposalsint | Nonedefault None

    Candidates each path proposes per level (defaults to width).
